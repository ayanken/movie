Final Destination


Introduction

Final Destination is one of my favorite horror movies. It is about a group of people who escape a terrible accident, but they soon discover that death is still coming for them.


Plot

The story begins when Alex Browning has a vision of a plane crashing. He warns his friends and some of them leave the plane before it takes off. The plane actually crashes, but the survivors soon realize that they cannot easily escape death.


Cast


Main Characters

The main characters include Alex Browning, Clear Rivers, Carter Horton, Billy Hitchcock, Terry Chaney, and Tod Waggner. Devon Sawa plays Alex Browning, while Ali Larter plays Clear Rivers.

Reviews

I like Final Destination because it is exciting, scary, and unpredictable. The movie keeps me interested because the characters try different ways to escape death. I would recommend this movie to people who enjoy horror and thriller movies.
